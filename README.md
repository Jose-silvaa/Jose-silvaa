### 🔎 About Me

I'm a **QA Engineer** focused on ensuring the quality and reliability of critical systems, currently working in the **Oil & Gas** sector at **Sapiensia Tecnologia**.

I own the full QA lifecycle — from reviewing requirements and writing test plans and test cases to executing functional, regression, smoke and exploratory tests, both manual and automated. I always aim to build strategic test plans that prioritize the highest-risk business scenarios and edge cases.

I enjoy working closely with developers and product teams, reporting inconsistencies with clear evidence and actively helping speed up the bug-fixing cycle. I believe **quality is the whole team's responsibility, not just QA's.**

I'm constantly growing technically, deepening my knowledge of test automation and QA best practices to help deliver increasingly reliable products.
